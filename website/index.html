FAQ

YOU ASKED.
WE ANSWER.

Who’s behind INFL8 Club?

Meet John and Julia.

With a combined 30+ years of experience in the entertainment industry and a shared passion for music, events and bringing people together, we created INFL8 Club.

Inspired by the unforgettable energy of old school house parties, INFL8 Club brings that same fun, music filled and social atmosphere to Melbourne with a fresh and exclusive twist.

But INFL8 Club is about more than just a night out. We are passionate about creating experiences for everyone, with events designed to bring together kids, families, friends and music lovers of all ages.

We love working with people, creating memorable experiences and building a community around great music, good energy and unforgettable moments. Whether you are bringing the family, catching up with friends or ready to relive those old school party vibes, we cannot wait to share INFL8 Club with Melbourne.

What is an inflatable nightclub?

It’s as straightforward as it sounds. It’s a blown up, supersized tent designed to be a nightclub right in your desired space.

Will there be a DJ at my event?

Yes! Unlike any other inflatable hire, INFL8 Club provides a resident DJ with 15+ years experience included in the price so there’s no annoying extra add-ons.. not exactly a ‘nightclub’ experience with a bluetooth speaker is it? 😉

Does the DJ take requests?

Absolutely. Even if the song was released yesterday, best believe we’ve got that song!

What areas do you service?

INFL8 is based around Melbourne, VIC and surrounding inner and outer suburbs. No travel fee, ever!

Is there an age limit?

No! INFL8 Club is for all ages, and is inclusive of everyone, because there is no age limit on fun! Parental supervision required for children under the age of 18.

What happens if it rains?

Melbourne is unpredictable with its weather. If your booking date lands on an extremely windy or rainy day, please rest assured that we will arrange for another date at no cost. Safety is our #1 priority, so we can’t take shortcuts. Although we don’t want to disappoint anyone, don’t worry.. the party will return!

Will there be ventilation?

On warmer days, INFL8 will provide fans inside to keep the area cool and ventilated.

Do I need to sign anything?

Yes. The Service Agreement secures the legalities and must be signed and returned no later than 5 business days before your event. If we don't have a signed agreement by that deadline, the booking may be cancelled.

How many people can it fit?

Safely, around 25 people can fit in the INFL8 Club at once.

Do you accept Afterpay?

Simple answer, we sure do!

Do you require a deposit?

Yes. To finalise your booking, a $100 deposit will be required. The remaining payment will be required 7 days prior to your event date. For our customers using Afterpay, full upfront payment will be required.

Do you have insurance?

Yes. INFL8 Club has full Public Liability insurance.
